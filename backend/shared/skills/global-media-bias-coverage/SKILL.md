# Global & APAC Media Bias Coverage Guidelines

## 1. Principles of Cross-Regional Balanced Coverage
To ensure grounded, comprehensive intelligence briefings that reflect diverse international viewpoints:
1. **Regional Representation**: Every comprehensive investigation must include primary reporting from outlets based in the primary nation of the event, affected neighboring economies, and major global wire/financial desks.
2. **AllSides-Aligned Ideological Spectrum**:
   - `LEFT`: Progressivism, state-led welfare, regulatory oversight, labor priority.
   - `LEAN_LEFT`: Institutional reform, social democratic perspective, multilateral agreements.
   - `CENTER`: Balanced wire reporting (AP, Reuters), equal quote weighting, procedural dispatches.
   - `LEAN_RIGHT`: Market-oriented economics, fiscal restraint, skepticism of administrative bloat.
   - `RIGHT`: National sovereignty, strict border policies, traditional conservatism.
3. **Factual Reliability Tiers**:
   - `TIER_1_WIRE`: Institutional wire services with rigorous verification desks (Reuters, AP, AFP, Kyodo).
   - `TIER_1_FINANCIAL`: Global financial papers with deep regulatory and market scrutiny (Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, Nikkei).
   - `TIER_2_NATIONAL`: Premier national newspapers of record with broad editorial bureaus.
   - `TIER_3_REGIONAL_SPECIALIZED`: Regional or domain-specific outlets with frontline jurisdictional insight.

## 2. Cross-Border Media Quotas for Breaking Stories
When ingesting articles for breaking topics:
- **Major Global Event**: Minimum 12-18 articles spanning at least 4 distinct geographical jurisdictions (e.g. US, UK/EU, East Asia/APAC, South Asia/Oceania).
- **APAC-Centric Event (e.g., Semiconductor policy, maritime trade, currency policy)**:
  - At least 3 East Asian outlets (Japan, South Korea, Taiwan/HK).
  - At least 2 South Asian / Southeast Asian outlets (India, Singapore).
  - At least 2 Australasian outlets (Australia, New Zealand).
  - At least 4 Global Wire & Financial desks for international comparative benchmarks.
