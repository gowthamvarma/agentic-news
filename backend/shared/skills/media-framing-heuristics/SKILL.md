# Media Framing & Political Lean Heuristics

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Political Spectrum
Analyze reporting across both economic and social policy axes:
- **Far Left / Left**: Structural critique of capital, prioritization of labor power, suspicion of corporate state collusion.
- **Center-Left**: Focus on institutional reform, regulated social democracy, public welfare investments.
- **Center**: Neutral wire protocol (Reuters/AP style), balancing attributed quotes, focus on procedural outcomes.
- **Center-Right**: Market deregulation, fiscal restraint, emphasis on private enterprise efficiency.
- **Right / Far Right**: Nationalism, border sovereignty, skepticism of multilateral treaties and administrative regulations.

## 2. Framing Techniques to Isolate
1. **Selection & Omission**: Which vital context or stakeholder response was excluded?
2. **Placement & Prominence**: Is a major caveat buried in the 18th paragraph while an sensational accusation leads the headline?
3. **Spin & Loaded Vocabulary**: Use of emotive verbs ("slammed", "capitulated", "conceded") instead of neutral reporting verbs ("stated", "announced").
4. **Headline-to-Body Dissonance (Clickbait)**: Does the body text support or contradict the dramatic claim made in the title?
