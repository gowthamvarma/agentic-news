# AI Builder Cup 2026

Information and guidelines sourced from [AI Builder Cup - Challenges & Themes](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html).

---

## 1. Hackathon Overview

The **Google Cloud AI Builder Cup** (Gen AI Academy APAC Edition) challenges builders across JAPAC to create impactful, functional AI prototypes powered by Google Cloud technologies.

### Key Requirements & Deliverables

- **Working Prototype**: Submissions must be a functional prototype that demonstrates a clear solution in action—not just a pitch deck or mockup.
- **Mandatory AI Stack**: Must leverage Google AI models (such as **Gemini** or **Gemma**) or be developed using agentic platforms (such as **Agent Platform**, **Antigravity**, or **AI Studio**).
- **Deployment Platform**: Solutions must be deployed on **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** using **Cloud Run** or **Firebase**.
- **Proposal Deck**: A PDF presentation detailing the project vision, problem addressed, community benefits, project feasibility, and scalability.
- **Demonstration Video**: A 3-minute video (public YouTube, Vimeo, or Google Drive link) demonstrating the AI solution in action.
- **Language**: All code, documentation, and presentations must be submitted in English.

## 2. Selected Theme

### Media, Content & Digital Experiences

> **Problem Statement**:
> Build an AI-powered solution that transforms how content is created, discovered, personalized, understood, or experienced. Solutions may address challenges across media, entertainment, publishing, education, communication, or other digital content ecosystems.

## 3. Judging Criteria

Submissions are evaluated against four weighted criteria:

### 1. Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation (40%)
- **Meaningful Use of Gen AI**: How effectively and meaningfully Generative AI has been incorporated into the solution, directly contributing to solving the problem or enhancing the overall outcome.
- **Technical Implementation**: The solution must be functional, technically sound, and appropriately implemented.
- **Scalability & Sustainability**: Demonstrates potential for wider adoption, technical scalability, and viability beyond the prototype phase.

### 2. Problem Alignment & Impact (25%)
- **Problem Alignment**: How effectively the solution addresses the chosen theme and its specific objectives.
- **Potential Impact**: Demonstrates clear, measurable, and meaningful value for intended users, organizations, industries, communities, or stakeholders.

### 3. Innovation & Creativity (25%)
- **Originality & Differentiation**: Demonstrates a fresh perspective, novel approach, or clear differentiation from existing solutions in the market.
- **Innovation in the Use of Gen AI**: Showcases a creative or sophisticated application of Generative AI beyond basic or superficial wrappers.

### 4. User Experience & Solution Design (10%)
- **Usability & Accessibility**: Intuitive, user-friendly, and accessible interface and workflow.
- **Experience & Integration**: Cohesive integration of all technical components (including Gen AI capabilities) into a smooth, cohesive user experience.
