# Build Agent Skills with Google

Imagine onboarding a brilliant new employee who somehow forgets everything overnight and has to reread the entire company handbook every single morning. Sometimes, this is what it feels like to work with AI.

Agent skills turn your AI into an elite, self-sufficient team member. It knows your business rules inside out and only reads the manual when needed.

---

## Course Overview

> ### Preview Video Transcript
> 
> Ever feel like you’re constantly repeating yourself with an AI agent? Pasting guidelines, addressing rules, and explaining constraints every session is exhausting, wastes tokens, and leads to unpredictable results. It’s like hiring a brilliant employee with zero domain knowledge; they have to read the entire company handbook every morning just to do their job.
> 
> That’s exactly why an AI agent needs skills. Think of a skill as a portable, plug-and-play handbook that bridges the gap between general AI capabilities and your specific business rules. These simple Markdown packages bundle instructions, references, and workflows together so your agents never start from a blank slate.
> 
> The best part? You don't have to write skills from scratch. An AI agent can help you package your expertise effortlessly. Even better, the agent uses **progressive disclosure** to load skills only when needed, saving valuable context tokens.
> 
> While writing a single skill is easy, scaling, sharing, and maintaining a library across an enterprise is the real challenge. That’s what you’ll master in this course using Google’s agent-building ecosystem.
