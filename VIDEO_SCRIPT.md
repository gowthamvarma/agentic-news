## 🎙️ Voiceover Delivery & Recording Tips

1. **Pacing**: Speak at a clear, energetic cadence (~140 wpm). Do not rush, especially during the Contradiction Matrix and Consequence Cascades sections.
2. **Emphasis**: Put vocal emphasis on contrast words: *“Instead of text compression... forensic crime scene”*, *“Not just symptoms... root causes”*.
3. **Cursor Movement**: Use smooth, intentional cursor glides. Avoid jerky mouse circles. Use subtle zoom-ins (110%-125%) on key UI elements like the confidence meters, contradiction badges, and audio play buttons.
4. **Theme Setting**: Recommended to record the demo using the **Dark** or **Cyberpunk** DaisyUI theme for maximum contrast and modern aesthetic appeal.
5. **Pre-Audited Test Case**: Bookmark the golden investigation link:
   `http://localhost:5173/analysis/09c0dbae-2b01-4852-83de-ddc89fdb6614` for instantaneous tab navigation without waiting during recording.
